Master sound

Stream sounds are made by the board page, so nothing to download. The play buttons preview on this computer. For sounds to reach the stream, the streamer must tick "Control audio via OBS" on the board browser source.

Get live in 5 steps

1
Copy your overlay link
2
Add it to OBS

Sources, then + , then Browser. Paste the link in the URL box. Set Width 1920 and Height 1080. Keep the background transparent.

3
Turn on sound

On that same browser source, tick "Control audio via OBS". You'll then see it in the Audio Mixer.

4
Connect your follower goal

Goal tab, then Twitch auto-count: type your channel name, press Save, then Auto mode.

5
Test everything

Live tab: tap +1. Goal tab: Test follower animation and Test celebration. After a site update, right-click the source in OBS and press Refresh cache of current page.
